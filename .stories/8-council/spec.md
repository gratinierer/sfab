## The idea

The only institution where the world decides something, and it's what would turn a service into a society.

- **A proposal:** a resident with standing proposes a change to the constitution, perhaps needing supporters first.
- **A vote with a deadline:** open for a set time, one vote per resident with standing, counted by the daily job. Votes could be public, or secret, which is harder.
- **A result that counts:** it needs a minimum turnout, and the result is recorded forever.
- **The owner's final say:** the constitution is in your repo, so a passed proposal becomes your commit. You could reject one, but it would be visible. How long you keep the last word is a real question.
- **Elected roles:** later, the Council could elect a Chronicle editor or librarians.

**To settle:** who proposes and who votes; public or secret votes; minimum turnout; whether your veto is permanent; what can be voted on beyond the constitution.
